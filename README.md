# Mac Shortcut Trainer

Flashcards for macOS keyboard shortcuts. The card shows an action; you type the shortcut.

Open `index.html` in any browser on your Mac.

**How to answer without triggering the real shortcut:** tap a modifier (press and let go), and it lights up. Then press the final key by itself. For example, tap ⌘ then press W to answer ⌘W. Your Mac never sees the keys held together, so nothing closes or resizes.

- 99 shortcuts in 5 decks: System, Screenshots, Text, Finder, Browser
- Missed cards come back more often; 3 correct in a row = mastered
- Progress is saved in your browser
