How to press shortcuts here without your real Mac reacting: tap the modifiers one at a time (press and let go), then press the last key. For ⌘W, tap ⌘, let go, press W. The keys never go down together, so macOS doesn't close, hide or resize anything. Lit modifiers show which ones are armed; tap again to disarm.

For fn / 🌐, click the on-screen fn key, since tapping the real one can open the emoji picker. Plain keys like space, arrows and return work as normal, and you can type text when a step asks for it. If the fake Mac gets into a mess, use Reset step.
