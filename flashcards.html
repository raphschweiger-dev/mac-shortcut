Why your screen won't jump around: never hold keys together.
  1. Press and release ⌘ (or ⇧ ⌥ ⌃). It lights up.
  2. Then press the final key on its own, like W.

The trainer records ⌘W, but macOS never sees them pressed together, so nothing closes, quits or goes fullscreen. Tap a lit modifier again to turn it off. For fn / 🌐, use the on-screen key, since tapping the real one can open the emoji picker. Wrong answers come back sooner; a card counts as mastered after 3 correct in a row.
